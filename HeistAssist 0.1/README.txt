BRANDON'S MENU 18 - COP CONTROLS, NO INTRO / OUTRO

Close PAYDAY 2. Extract this ZIP into your existing PAYDAY 2/mods/HeistAssist
folder and replace files, including assets. Keep one installed copy.
Launch the game. F5 > Protection contains two separate switches:

COPS DO NOT SHOOT (SOLO / HOST)
Disables firearm permission for hostile cops, including existing enemies.
Applies to all players in your hosted game. Turning OFF restores the last
fire permission requested by the AI. Allied bots and converted cops are excluded.
This is a firearm control, not immunity from melee, tasers or scripted attacks.

AUTO-KILL NEWLY SPAWNED COPS (SOLO / HOST)
Kills new hostile enemies on the next game update after registration.
Existing cops are not killed when you enable it. Turning OFF stops new kills.
Invulnerable mission characters are not forcibly killed. Civilians and allies
are excluded. Deaths can still trigger alarms, pagers or mission events.

Both switches default OFF, persist with settings, and are included in custom
presets. Normal play / All assists OFF disables both. Neither acts as a client.
Colors and the compact artwork menu are retained. No intro/outro is loaded.
Requires SuperBLT and BeardLib; Carry Stacker remains optional for multi-bags.

Validation: manifest/ZIP/asset integrity and static code checks. Game behavior
needs a solo/host test; this environment cannot run a PAYDAY 2 heist.
