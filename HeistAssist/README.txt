BRANDON'S MENU 13.1 - FULL COLOR FIX, NO INTRO / OUTRO

Close PAYDAY 2. Replace the files in your existing mods/HeistAssist folder,
including assets. Restart. F5 opens the menu directly. No GIFs are loaded.
Do not create another installed copy under a second folder name.

COLORS
F5 > Appearance > RESET TO BLUE restores visible color, then choose Hue,
saturation, brightness or a six-digit HEX color and press Enter.
Rainbow restores saturation/brightness from gray/black when enabled.
KEEP CURRENT COLOR turns cycling off. Eight favorite colors can be saved.
The artwork, borders, switches, slider accents and highlights are recolored.
Corrected the invalid BeardLib menu.my_items lookup to the supported Items().
Saturation 0 is gray; brightness 0 is black. They remain available when cycling
is off. Adjusting Hue restores visible color if you were on gray/black.

GAMEPLAY
24 existing switches plus damage, speed and bag controls are retained.
Host-only features still require solo / hosting. Carry Stacker is required for
multiple bags. Device timer options do not cover every mission-script delay.
Settings remain in mods/saves/brandons_new_menu.json.

VALIDATION

calls and files in this package, texture presence, manifest paths and ZIP integrity.
